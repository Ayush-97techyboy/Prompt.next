WHY WE WERE FOUNDED

This pattern repeats often enough to be structural, not incidental. BCG's 2024 research, comparing results to its own study a decade earlier, found that more than two-thirds of large-scale technology programmes are still not delivered on time, within budget, or within their planned scope, a figure essentially unchanged in ten years.

Brilliant strategies. Approved budgets. Capable teams. Credible vendors. And still, far too many of them fail to deliver what was promised.

The cause, almost without exception, is structural. Nobody is accountable for the whole. Multiple parties are accountable for their own piece, and the gaps between those pieces are where programmes go to fail.

Prompt Next was founded to close that structural gap, not by adding another layer of advice, but by building a network and a methodology designed specifically to take the accountability that was missing.
