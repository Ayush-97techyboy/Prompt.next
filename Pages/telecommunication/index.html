straightforward. The environment does not allow for a clean
start.

When Delivery Does Not Match the Commitment

A major telecommunications company awarded a large-
scale network modernisation and customer platform
programme to a global technology services firm. The
proposal presented deep expertise, dedicated leadership,
and a structured delivery approach. What arrived at
execution stage was a materially different team, more
junior, less specialised, and supported by subcontracted
resource with limited familiarity with the client's existing
infrastructure.

This is a structural feature of how large delivery
organisations operate, not an isolated lapse. Senior
expertise wins engagements. Execution is resourced
differently. By the time the gap becomes apparent, the
contract is signed and the cost of changing direction is
significant.
