Industry Context

Telecom operators across Asia-Pacific are running the most complex technology agendas in the region. Legacy infrastructure that was never designed for modern data demands. Customer platforms that need to scale without friction. And AI and automation being layered onto networks built decades before either existed. The work is not straightforward. The environment does not allow for a clean start.

When Delivery Does Not Match the Commitment

A major telecommunications company awarded a large-scale network modernisation and customer platform programme to a global technology services firm. The proposal presented deep expertise, dedicated leadership, and a structured delivery approach. What arrived at execution stage was a materially different team, more junior, less specialised, and supported by subcontracted resource with limited familiarity with the client's existing infrastructure.

This is a structural feature of how large delivery organisations operate, not an isolated lapse. Senior expertise wins engagements. Execution is resourced differently. By the time the gap becomes apparent, the contract is signed and the cost of changing direction is significant.
